# 30-second product ad

**0-4s:** Show relatable problem.

**4-10s:** Introduce product.

**10-20s:** Three short demonstration shots.

**20-26s:** Show supported proof or accurate feature details.

**26-30s:** Packshot and one CTA.

**Example:** Travel suitcase with realistic packing, zipper and wheel demonstrations. Use exact model reference photos and verified product dimensions.
