# 15-second product ad

**0-3s:** Close-up visual hook.

**3-8s:** Real product use.

**8-12s:** Verified feature demonstration.

**12-15s:** Packshot and CTA.

**Example:** Insulated water bottle. Begin with condensation macro, cut to commuter placing bottle in bag, show actual lid mechanism, end with accurate front-facing packshot. Generate each shot separately and edit together.
