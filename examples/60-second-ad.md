# 60-second product explainer

**0-5s:** Hook.

**5-15s:** Define customer problem.

**15-40s:** Step-by-step demonstration.

**40-52s:** Supported evidence or user benefit.

**52-60s:** Clear CTA.

**Example:** SaaS dashboard. Use authentic screen recordings for UI details. Generate only background footage and transitional scenes. Do not fabricate metrics or testimonials.
