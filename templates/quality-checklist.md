# Video review checklist

- [ ] **Product accuracy:** Shape, color, buttons and label match references.
- [ ] **Visual continuity:** Lighting, camera direction and materials remain stable.
- [ ] **Physics:** No unnatural clipping, gravity or deformation.
- [ ] **Branding:** Only approved logos, claims and pricing.
- [ ] **People:** Necessary consent for likeness and voice.
- [ ] **Audio:** Dialogue is clear and music is licensed.
- [ ] **Compliance:** Required disclosures and advertising rules reviewed.
- [ ] **Export:** Aspect ratio, resolution and safe zones checked.
- [ ] **Final playback:** Full exported video reviewed before publishing.
