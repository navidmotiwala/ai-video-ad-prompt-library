## Prompt example

**Product:** Perfume | **Style:** Luxury | **Format:** 9:16 | **Duration:** 8 seconds

```text
A crystal perfume bottle stands on black marble. A narrow golden
spotlight travels across its cap as the camera moves slowly around
the bottle. Soft vapor drifts behind it. Maintain the exact bottle
shape and label. End with a front-facing product hero shot. Luxury
studio lighting, realistic reflections, 9:16, eight seconds.
```
