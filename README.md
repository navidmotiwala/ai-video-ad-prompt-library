# AI Video Product Advertising Prompt Library

**Edition: 30 October 2026**

Reusable prompt codes, video ad recipes, shot grammar, camera movements, industry examples, workflows, quality checks and resources. Slash commands are a custom shorthand system, not native commands of any video generator.

**Usage:** Pick a recipe, replace product specifics, expand slash codes into plain language, then generate short shots and edit them together. Verify all commercial claims and branding.

## Core prompt grammar

Template: [product identity] + [advertising objective] + [scene] + [product action] + [camera movement] + [lighting] + [material behavior] + [timing] + [sound] + [output format] + [continuity rules].
Example: A premium matte-black wireless headphone rests on a dark stone pedestal. The camera slowly dollies forward while a narrow rim light reveals the curved earcups. Fine atmospheric haze moves behind the product. At second four the headphones rotate 30 degrees, exposing the controls. Natural studio reflections, accurate geometry, luxury commercial finish, 9:16, eight seconds. Soft mechanical click and restrained electronic ambience. Preserve product proportions and branding.

## Product ad prompt recipes

1. PERFUME: A crystal perfume bottle stands on black marble. A narrow golden spotlight travels across its engraved cap as the camera completes a slow half orbit. Wisps of translucent vapor move behind the bottle. Finish on a front-facing packshot with clear label visibility. Luxury fragrance campaign, eight seconds, 9:16, restrained sound.
2. SKINCARE: A serum bottle rests on pale limestone. A translucent droplet slides down the glass while soft daylight reveals the liquid texture. Macro push-in, clean clinical styling, no unverified efficacy claims, six seconds.
3. WATCH: Extreme macro of a mechanical watch crown, then a controlled rack focus to the dial. Second hand moves naturally. Brushed metal reflections, black studio background, luxury horology commercial, eight seconds.
4. SNEAKERS: A running shoe lands on a clean studio platform. Camera follows the sole flex and then orbits the heel, revealing tread details without altering the model. Crisp impact sound, sports campaign, eight seconds.
5. SMARTPHONE: A smartphone floats above a graphite pedestal, rotating slowly to show its edges, cameras and screen. Use a verified screen image in compositing, accurate camera count, high-end tech launch.
6. HEADPHONES: Premium headphones lift gently from a pedestal as the camera tracks along the earcup and headband. Soft rim light defines cushions and metallic controls. End on clean product hero.
7. CAR: A premium electric vehicle drives along a coastal road at dawn. Low tracking shot shows wheel motion, then a wide aerial reveals the road. Accurate car proportions, natural reflections, cinematic automotive spot.
8. COFFEE: Roasted coffee beans tumble in slow motion into a grinder. Cut to espresso pouring into a ceramic cup with realistic crema. Warm café lighting and close foley.
9. CHOCOLATE: A dark chocolate bar snaps in close macro. Tiny fragments fall naturally. A smooth ribbon of chocolate pours behind the packshot. Rich brown palette, tactile sound.
10. BURGER: A fresh burger is assembled layer by layer in a controlled overhead shot. Steam rises from the patty, sauce moves naturally. Finish on an appetizing hero shot with accurate ingredients.
11. SODA: Cold beverage can rotates as condensation droplets travel down its surface. Ice cubes splash into clear water behind it. Crisp fizz, cool blue studio lighting.
12. JEWELRY: A gold necklace rests on dark velvet. Slow camera slide follows the chain to the pendant as a small specular highlight traces the metal. Elegant restrained movement.
13. HANDBAG: A leather handbag sits on a sculptural platform. Camera passes close to stitching and hardware, then widens to the full silhouette. Maintain seams, handles and logo placement.
14. FURNITURE: A modern chair appears in a sunlit interior. Camera moves from its wood grain to a full room composition. A person sits naturally to show scale and function.
15. LAPTOP: Laptop opens on a clean desk. Camera tracks the hinge and keyboard, then shows a verified screen recording composited into the display. Natural office light.
16. CAMERA: A mirrorless camera rotates on a matte stage. Macro details show the lens mount, grip and dials. Mechanical click accents accompany a clean product reveal.
17. FITNESS BOTTLE: An insulated water bottle stands on a rocky trail. Fine water droplets collect on the exterior as sunrise moves across the background. Outdoor performance look without invented temperature claims.
18. COSMETICS: A lipstick twists upward from its case in a close studio shot. Smooth controlled motion reveals the finish. Cut to a swatch on a neutral surface with color consistency.
19. HOME APPLIANCE: A vacuum cleaner moves across a visible patch of debris in a realistic home. Locked camera before-and-after demonstration with consistent flooring and verified product features.
20. SOFTWARE: A founder works at a laptop while a real interface screen recording appears in a clean overlay. Cut between workflow steps and a concise verified benefit, professional B2B style.
21. REAL ESTATE: Morning sunlight fills a furnished apartment. Slow gimbal walk from living room to balcony, natural spatial continuity, no invented amenities.
22. HOTEL: A traveler enters a bright hotel suite. Camera glides toward the window and reveals the view. Relaxed hospitality campaign, use property-accurate visuals.
23. TRAVEL BAG: A suitcase rolls smoothly through an airport terminal. Low tracking shot follows wheels, then cut to organized interior compartments. Preserve product shape.
24. GAMING DEVICE: A gaming controller rotates above a dark platform with controlled RGB reflections. Camera pushes into triggers and thumbsticks, then reveals the full controller.
25. TOOTHBRUSH: An electric toothbrush rests beside a sink. Macro shot shows brush head movement and water droplets. Bright clean bathroom scene, no unsupported dental claims.
26. EYEWEAR: Sunglasses rotate in direct afternoon sunlight, showing frame contours and lens reflections. Smooth transition to a lifestyle shot of a model wearing the exact frame.
27. PET PRODUCT: A dog approaches a toy in a sunlit room, interacts with it naturally, and rests beside it. Authentic pet behavior, no unsafe handling.
28. KITCHENWARE: A chef places vegetables into a pan. Close view of natural sizzling, gentle steam and utensil movement. Product clearly visible, documentary food commercial.
29. MEDICAL DEVICE: A verified device is shown in a clinical setting with accurate controls and safe operation. Neutral professional lighting, no invented outcomes.
30. EDUCATION APP: Student completes a lesson on a phone. Camera shifts from focused expression to a real interface insert. End with a clear value proposition supported by actual product functionality.

## Ad concepts and funnels

Awareness: pattern interrupt, problem setup, product reveal, brand memory.
Consideration: product demo, objection handling, comparison, testimonials with permission, feature explanation, use-case montage.
Conversion: offer reveal, verified price, clear CTA, product close-up, deadline only if real.
Retention: onboarding tip, new feature walkthrough, usage reminder, cross-sell with relevant value.
Advocacy: authentic customer story, review montage with permission, creator collaboration, referral explainer.
Formats: six-second bumper; eight-second visual hook; fifteen-second short; thirty-second social spot; forty-five-second demo; sixty-second explainer; six-shot storyboard; looping digital signage; silent retail display; vertical story; shoppable video; marketplace listing video; live commerce opener; localized multi-language master.

## Video storytelling patterns

HOOK-DEMO-PROOF-CTA: unusual first frame, one useful demo, supported proof, clean end card.
PROBLEM-SOLUTION: show everyday friction, introduce product, show credible improvement.
UNBOX-USE-REACT: package opening, practical usage, genuine reaction.
QUESTION-ANSWER: on-screen question added in post, visual demonstration, concise answer.
MYTH-FACT: make the misconception explicit and present a verified correction.
THREE-FEATURES: one scene per benefit, same product continuity, final packshot.
BEFORE-AFTER: matching angle and conditions, accurate and nonmisleading difference.
DAY-IN-LIFE: show product used naturally across a short sequence.
CRAFTSMANSHIP: macro materials, hands at work, quality details.
SENSORY: close textures, fluid behavior, realistic foley.
SOCIAL-PROOF: approved customer footage, authentic attribution, factual claims.
COMPARISON: matched test conditions and documented criteria.
TEASER: silhouette, partial reveal, date added later in editing.
LAUNCH: hero reveal, key differentiator, clear availability and CTA.
EDUCATIONAL: teach a small useful concept and connect it naturally to the product.

## Advanced production workflows

WORKFLOW A TEXT-TO-VIDEO: define objective and target audience; select one scene; write physical actions; choose framing, lens and lighting; specify duration and aspect ratio; generate variations; review geometry and motion; edit; add typography and licensed sound; export.
WORKFLOW B IMAGE-TO-VIDEO: photograph or render exact product; clean source image; lock label and geometry; prompt primarily motion; animate one action per shot; check drift frame by frame; composite verified brand assets.
WORKFLOW C PRODUCT-TO-VIDEO: shoot product from front, back, side and macro; create reference sheet; write shot list; generate each shot separately; edit matching angles; verify materials and logos; finalize packshot.
WORKFLOW D UGC: write natural hook; film or generate disclosed creator footage; demonstrate real product use; record authentic narration; add captions; avoid fake testimonials.
WORKFLOW E MULTI-SHOT: write beat sheet; create consistent character/product references; generate opening and closing frames for each shot; produce individual clips; match lighting and screen direction; edit with audio continuity.
WORKFLOW F LOCALIZATION: export textless master; replace voiceover, captions, pricing, offers and regulatory text with approved localized versions; verify cultural context.
WORKFLOW G A/B TEST: hold audience and offer constant; vary only hook, framing or first-three-second action; compare thumb-stop, watch time, click-through and conversion using sufficient sample size.
WORKFLOW H AUTOMATION: keep structured shot briefs in CSV or JSON; batch render where platform permits; log seed, model, prompt, source assets and license; review outputs manually; render through editor.
WORKFLOW I QUALITY CONTROL: verify product geometry, hands, text, logos, shadows, reflections, physics, continuity, frame-safe regions, audio sync, disclosure and claims before publishing.

## Safety, accuracy and commercial checks

Do not invent prices, product specifications, reviews, medical results, financial returns, safety certifications or test data. Use approved trademarks and product imagery. Obtain appropriate consent for likeness and voice. Disclose synthetic content where required. Verify platform policies, music and stock asset licenses. Avoid misleading before-and-after transformations. Review local advertising rules, especially for health, finance, children, regulated products and political advertising. Add legal disclaimers in editing rather than trusting generated tiny text. No prompt guarantees exact geometry, typography, duration, audio or native tool support.
