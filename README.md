# AI Video Ad Prompt Library

**30 practical guides, reusable prompts, custom slash commands and production templates.**

A reference library for planning, generating, editing and testing AI product advertisements.

> **Important:** Slash commands are custom prompt shortcuts, not official commands in Sora, Veo, Runway, Kling or other video platforms. Expand them into descriptive instructions before generating.

## Quick start

1. [Learn the prompt formula](guides/01-getting-started.md).
2. [Find slash commands](guides/02-slash-commands.md) and [ready-made combinations](guides/03-combination-shortcuts.md).
3. [Copy a product prompt](guides/04-product-video-prompts.md) or choose a [specialist prompt](prompts/).
4. [Plan a storyboard](templates/storyboard.md) and [shot list](templates/shot-list.md).
5. Generate individual shots, edit them together and use the [quality checklist](templates/quality-checklist.md).

## Copyable example

**Product:** Perfume | **Style:** Luxury | **Duration:** 8 seconds | **Format:** 9:16

```text
A crystal perfume bottle stands on black marble. The camera moves slowly
around the bottle as a narrow golden highlight reveals its cap. Thin
vapor moves behind the product without covering the label. Preserve
exact bottle geometry and branding. End with a front-facing packshot.
Realistic studio reflections, eight seconds, vertical 9:16.
```

## Production rules

- **Accuracy:** Preserve real product geometry, packaging and labels.
- **Claims:** Verify specifications, prices, testimonials and performance statements.
- **Branding:** Add approved text, logos and UI in post-production.
- **Rights:** Obtain appropriate consent and licenses for people, voices and music.
- **Platform support:** Check current capabilities and output limits in official documentation.
- **Links:** No UTM tracking parameters in resource URLs.
