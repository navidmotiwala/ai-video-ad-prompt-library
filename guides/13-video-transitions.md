# Video transition library

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for video transition library in AI video advertising.

## 1. Match cut

**How to use:** Cut between shots with similar shapes and framing.

**Example:** Round watch dial becomes a round car wheel.

## 2. Object wipe

**How to use:** Foreground object covers frame and reveals next scene.

**Example:** Shopping bag sweeps across lens into storefront.

## 3. Whip pan

**How to use:** Fast camera pan conceals edit.

**Example:** Pan from kitchen counter to dining table.

## 4. Hidden cut

**How to use:** Use darkness or occlusion to mask a scene change.

**Example:** Camera passes behind pillar and enters a new room.

## 5. Speed-ramp cut

**How to use:** Accelerate toward a cut and slow after it.

**Example:** Sneaker jumps, camera rushes forward, landing in running track.

## 6. Reflection transition

**How to use:** Move from reflected scene to real scene.

**Example:** Sunglasses reflection becomes a beach establishing shot.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using match cut.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
