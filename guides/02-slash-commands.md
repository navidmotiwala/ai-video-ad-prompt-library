# Slash commands

**Important:** These are custom shorthand codes, not official platform commands. These are not native commands supported by every AI video model. Expand a shortcut into a descriptive prompt before generating.

## Product

- **`/hero-shot`**: product centered as visual hero
- **`/product-reveal`**: controlled unveiling
- **`/packshot`**: clear packaging and brand-facing final frame
- **`/floating-product`**: weightless product motion
- **`/levitation`**: product rises gently
- **`/unboxing`**: packaging opens sequentially
- **`/exploded-view`**: components separate in ordered layers
- **`/assembly`**: parts assemble accurately
- **`/disassembly`**: parts separate without deformation
- **`/cutaway`**: internal mechanism visible
- **`/cross-section`**: material layers revealed
- **`/texture-pass`**: light scans product texture
- **`/material-swap`**: deliberate material transition
- **`/colorways`**: same model shown in multiple colors
- **`/size-comparison`**: scaled objects shown side by side
- **`/feature-highlight`**: one feature receives attention
- **`/before-after`**: comparable transformation
- **`/benefit-demo`**: practical use demonstrates benefit
- **`/proof-shot`**: visible supporting evidence
- **`/cta-endcard`**: final clean product and call to action
- **`/logo-safe`**: reserve clear space for verified logo added in editing.

## Camera

- **`/macro-orbit`**: close camera circles product
- **`/dolly-in`**: camera advances
- **`/dolly-out`**: camera retreats
- **`/truck-left`**: camera moves left
- **`/truck-right`**: camera moves right
- **`/pedestal-up`**: camera rises vertically
- **`/pedestal-down`**: camera descends
- **`/crane`**: elevated sweeping move
- **`/orbit-180`**: half-circle orbit
- **`/orbit-360`**: full orbit
- **`/push-pull`**: controlled forward and backward motion
- **`/rack-focus`**: shift focal plane
- **`/top-down`**: overhead view
- **`/low-angle`**: below subject
- **`/high-angle`**: above subject
- **`/pov`**: first-person view
- **`/over-shoulder`**: frame past shoulder
- **`/tracking`**: follow subject
- **`/handheld`**: restrained operator movement
- **`/locked-off`**: fixed camera
- **`/whip-pan`**: rapid pan transition
- **`/snap-zoom`**: quick zoom
- **`/parallax`**: layered depth shift
- **`/tilt-up`**: upward camera tilt
- **`/tilt-down`**: downward tilt
- **`/roll`**: camera rotates around lens axis
- **`/fpv`**: agile first-person flight
- **`/drone-reveal`**: aerial move exposes scene
- **`/match-cut`**: visually matched edit between shots.

## Motion

- **`/slow-motion`**: slowed realistic action
- **`/speed-ramp`**: acceleration or deceleration
- **`/freeze-frame`**: apparent stopped action
- **`/time-lapse`**: compressed duration
- **`/hyperlapse`**: moving time-lapse
- **`/loop`**: matching first and last pose
- **`/seamless-loop`**: no visible restart
- **`/morph`**: gradual controlled transformation
- **`/particle-reveal`**: particles expose product
- **`/liquid-splash`**: fluid impact
- **`/smoke-reveal`**: drifting smoke uncovers object
- **`/dust-burst`**: airborne dust impact
- **`/fabric-flow`**: cloth motion
- **`/water-droplets`**: realistic droplets
- **`/condensation`**: droplets on cold surface
- **`/ice-crack`**: visible fracture
- **`/shatter`**: material breaks
- **`/magnetic-assembly`**: components converge
- **`/kinetic-type`**: moving typography added in post
- **`/stop-motion`**: stepped physical animation
- **`/claymation`**: clay stop motion aesthetic
- **`/paper-cut`**: layered paper movement

## Audio

- **`/foley`**: real-world object sound

- **`/whoosh`**: directional transition sound
- **`/impact`**: emphasis hit
- **`/room-tone`**: subtle environmental ambience
- **`/voiceover`**: narrated script
