## Ad concepts and funnels

Awareness: pattern interrupt, problem setup, product reveal, brand memory.
Consideration: product demo, objection handling, comparison, testimonials with permission, feature explanation, use-case montage.
Conversion: offer reveal, verified price, clear CTA, product close-up, deadline only if real.
Retention: onboarding tip, new feature walkthrough, usage reminder, cross-sell with relevant value.
Advocacy: authentic customer story, review montage with permission, creator collaboration, referral explainer.
Formats: six-second bumper; eight-second visual hook; fifteen-second short; thirty-second social spot; forty-five-second demo; sixty-second explainer; six-shot storyboard; looping digital signage; silent retail display; vertical story; shoppable video; marketplace listing video; live commerce opener; localized multi-language master.
