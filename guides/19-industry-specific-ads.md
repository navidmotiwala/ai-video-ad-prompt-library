# Industry-specific advertising prompts

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for industry-specific advertising prompts in AI video advertising.

## 1. Automotive

**How to use:** Low tracking shot follows a car on a coastal road; reveal grille and wheel details without changing model geometry.

**Example:** Maintain road safety and accurate car features.

## 2. Real estate

**How to use:** Continuous walkthrough of a real furnished apartment from entry to balcony.

**Example:** Do not invent rooms, amenities or views.

## 3. BFSI

**How to use:** Customer uses an actual finance app interface in a calm everyday setting.

**Example:** Avoid unverified returns or promises.

## 4. Healthcare

**How to use:** Show a real device in a clinical environment with accurate handling.

**Example:** Avoid unsupported treatment claims.

## 5. Fashion

**How to use:** Model walks naturally in a studio while fabric movement reveals tailoring.

**Example:** Preserve garment cut and color.

## 6. Beauty

**How to use:** Macro serum texture and controlled product reveal on limestone.

**Example:** No unsupported efficacy claims.

## 7. Electronics

**How to use:** Detailed orbit around a phone with accurate ports, buttons and camera layout.

**Example:** Composite real UI screens.

## 8. SaaS

**How to use:** Show a real dashboard recording inside a professional work scene.

**Example:** Use verified feature and performance claims.

## 9. FMCG

**How to use:** Cold beverage packshot with realistic condensation and splash.

**Example:** Use actual packaging.

## 10. Education

**How to use:** Student completes a real lesson on screen in a natural environment.

**Example:** Show real product functionality.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using automotive.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
