# AI video audio engineering

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for ai video audio engineering in AI video advertising.

## 1. Foley

**How to use:** Capture or synthesize object-specific sound aligned with contact.

**Example:** Packaging opening and mechanical clicks.

## 2. Voiceover

**How to use:** Write a short spoken script with clear pacing and product claims.

**Example:** 15-second explainer with approved copy.

## 3. Lip sync

**How to use:** Use authorized voice and likeness; verify mouth timing.

**Example:** Consented creator demo.

## 4. Music

**How to use:** Use appropriately licensed music with clean edits.

**Example:** Soft electronic bed under tech product.

## 5. Mixing

**How to use:** Prioritize voice clarity; duck music under dialogue.

**Example:** Keep CTA audible.

## 6. Localization

**How to use:** Translate and review script, timing, pronunciation and legal copy.

**Example:** Hindi, English and Arabic ad variants.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using foley.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
