# UGC and influencer advertising

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for ugc and influencer advertising in AI video advertising.

## 1. First-use demo

**How to use:** Creator opens package, demonstrates one real feature and gives a grounded reaction.

**Example:** Use actual product and authentic experience.

## 2. Problem-first

**How to use:** Creator describes a relatable frustration, then demonstrates the product.

**Example:** Do not fabricate testimonials.

## 3. Day-in-life

**How to use:** Show the product used naturally across morning, commute and evening.

**Example:** Keep continuity across clips.

## 4. Comparison

**How to use:** Creator compares two products under identical conditions.

**Example:** Document test criteria and permissions.

## 5. FAQ response

**How to use:** Creator answers a common customer question with a short visual demo.

**Example:** Avoid unsupported claims.

## 6. Voiceover script

**How to use:** Hook: Here is how I use [product]. Demo: show one step. Close: See details at [destination]..

**Example:** Use a licensed or consented voice.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using first-use demo.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
