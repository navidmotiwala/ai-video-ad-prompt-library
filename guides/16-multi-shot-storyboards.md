# Multi-shot video storyboards

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for multi-shot video storyboards in AI video advertising.

## 1. 6-second bumper

**How to use:** 0-2s: striking product close-up; 2-4s: one key action; 4-6s: packshot and CTA.

**Example:** Premium headphones: earcup detail, fold mechanism, final product.

## 2. 15-second short

**How to use:** 0-3s: hook; 3-8s: demo; 8-12s: benefit; 12-15s: CTA.

**Example:** Insulated bottle: ice reveal, commute use, product close-up, end card.

## 3. 30-second social ad

**How to use:** 0-4s: problem; 4-10s: reveal; 10-20s: three demo shots; 20-26s: verified proof; 26-30s: CTA.

**Example:** Travel suitcase: packing friction, organized interior, wheel demo, product close.

## 4. 60-second explainer

**How to use:** 0-5s: hook; 5-15s: problem; 15-40s: walkthrough; 40-52s: supported evidence; 52-60s: CTA.

**Example:** SaaS dashboard: workflow pain, real screen demo, verified customer outcome.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using 6-second bumper.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
