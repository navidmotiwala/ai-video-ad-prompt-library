## Product ad prompt recipes

### 1. PERFUME

A crystal perfume bottle stands on black marble. A narrow golden spotlight travels across its engraved cap as the camera completes a slow half orbit. Wisps of translucent vapor move behind the bottle. Finish on a front-facing packshot with clear label visibility. Luxury fragrance campaign, eight seconds, 9:16, restrained sound.
### 2. SKINCARE

A serum bottle rests on pale limestone. A translucent droplet slides down the glass while soft daylight reveals the liquid texture. Macro push-in, clean clinical styling, no unverified efficacy claims, six seconds.
### 3. WATCH

Extreme macro of a mechanical watch crown, then a controlled rack focus to the dial. Second hand moves naturally. Brushed metal reflections, black studio background, luxury horology commercial, eight seconds.
### 4. SNEAKERS

A running shoe lands on a clean studio platform. Camera follows the sole flex and then orbits the heel, revealing tread details without altering the model. Crisp impact sound, sports campaign, eight seconds.
### 5. SMARTPHONE

A smartphone floats above a graphite pedestal, rotating slowly to show its edges, cameras and screen. Use a verified screen image in compositing, accurate camera count, high-end tech launch.
### 6. HEADPHONES

Premium headphones lift gently from a pedestal as the camera tracks along the earcup and headband. Soft rim light defines cushions and metallic controls. End on clean product hero.
### 7. CAR

A premium electric vehicle drives along a coastal road at dawn. Low tracking shot shows wheel motion, then a wide aerial reveals the road. Accurate car proportions, natural reflections, cinematic automotive spot.
### 8. COFFEE

Roasted coffee beans tumble in slow motion into a grinder. Cut to espresso pouring into a ceramic cup with realistic crema. Warm café lighting and close foley.
### 9. CHOCOLATE

A dark chocolate bar snaps in close macro. Tiny fragments fall naturally. A smooth ribbon of chocolate pours behind the packshot. Rich brown palette, tactile sound.
### 10. BURGER

A fresh burger is assembled layer by layer in a controlled overhead shot. Steam rises from the patty, sauce moves naturally. Finish on an appetizing hero shot with accurate ingredients.
### 11. SODA

Cold beverage can rotates as condensation droplets travel down its surface. Ice cubes splash into clear water behind it. Crisp fizz, cool blue studio lighting.
### 12. JEWELRY

A gold necklace rests on dark velvet. Slow camera slide follows the chain to the pendant as a small specular highlight traces the metal. Elegant restrained movement.
### 13. HANDBAG

A leather handbag sits on a sculptural platform. Camera passes close to stitching and hardware, then widens to the full silhouette. Maintain seams, handles and logo placement.
### 14. FURNITURE

A modern chair appears in a sunlit interior. Camera moves from its wood grain to a full room composition. A person sits naturally to show scale and function.
### 15. LAPTOP

Laptop opens on a clean desk. Camera tracks the hinge and keyboard, then shows a verified screen recording composited into the display. Natural office light.
### 16. CAMERA

A mirrorless camera rotates on a matte stage. Macro details show the lens mount, grip and dials. Mechanical click accents accompany a clean product reveal.
### 17. FITNESS BOTTLE

An insulated water bottle stands on a rocky trail. Fine water droplets collect on the exterior as sunrise moves across the background. Outdoor performance look without invented temperature claims.
### 18. COSMETICS

A lipstick twists upward from its case in a close studio shot. Smooth controlled motion reveals the finish. Cut to a swatch on a neutral surface with color consistency.
### 19. HOME APPLIANCE

A vacuum cleaner moves across a visible patch of debris in a realistic home. Locked camera before-and-after demonstration with consistent flooring and verified product features.
### 20. SOFTWARE

A founder works at a laptop while a real interface screen recording appears in a clean overlay. Cut between workflow steps and a concise verified benefit, professional B2B style.
### 21. REAL ESTATE

Morning sunlight fills a furnished apartment. Slow gimbal walk from living room to balcony, natural spatial continuity, no invented amenities.
### 22. HOTEL

A traveler enters a bright hotel suite. Camera glides toward the window and reveals the view. Relaxed hospitality campaign, use property-accurate visuals.
### 23. TRAVEL BAG

A suitcase rolls smoothly through an airport terminal. Low tracking shot follows wheels, then cut to organized interior compartments. Preserve product shape.
### 24. GAMING DEVICE

A gaming controller rotates above a dark platform with controlled RGB reflections. Camera pushes into triggers and thumbsticks, then reveals the full controller.
### 25. TOOTHBRUSH

An electric toothbrush rests beside a sink. Macro shot shows brush head movement and water droplets. Bright clean bathroom scene, no unsupported dental claims.
### 26. EYEWEAR

Sunglasses rotate in direct afternoon sunlight, showing frame contours and lens reflections. Smooth transition to a lifestyle shot of a model wearing the exact frame.
### 27. PET PRODUCT

A dog approaches a toy in a sunlit room, interacts with it naturally, and rests beside it. Authentic pet behavior, no unsafe handling.
### 28. KITCHENWARE

A chef places vegetables into a pan. Close view of natural sizzling, gentle steam and utensil movement. Product clearly visible, documentary food commercial.
### 29. MEDICAL DEVICE

A verified device is shown in a clinical setting with accurate controls and safe operation. Neutral professional lighting, no invented outcomes.
### 30. EDUCATION APP

Student completes a lesson on a phone. Camera shifts from focused expression to a real interface insert. End with a clear value proposition supported by actual product functionality.
