**Purpose:** Practical reference for social platform ad formats in AI video advertising.

## 1. Instagram Reels

**How to use:** Prioritize vertical framing, strong first seconds and safe zones for interface overlays.

**Example:** Check current Meta placement specs.

## 2. YouTube Shorts

**How to use:** Build a fast hook, clear subject and readable post-produced captions.

**Example:** Check current YouTube upload specs.

## 3. TikTok

**How to use:** Use native-feeling demonstrations and platform-safe audio.

**Example:** Check TikTok creative and ad policies.

## 4. LinkedIn

**How to use:** Lead with the business problem and real workflow proof.

**Example:** Avoid unverifiable metrics.

## 5. Marketplace video

**How to use:** Show exact product dimensions, packaging and real use.

**Example:** Check marketplace media rules.

## 6. Digital signage

**How to use:** Use silent-first visuals, strong product recognition and legible end cards.

**Example:** Export for actual screen dimensions.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using instagram reels.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
