## Video storytelling patterns

HOOK-DEMO-PROOF-CTA: unusual first frame, one useful demo, supported proof, clean end card.
PROBLEM-SOLUTION: show everyday friction, introduce product, show credible improvement.
UNBOX-USE-REACT: package opening, practical usage, genuine reaction.
QUESTION-ANSWER: on-screen question added in post, visual demonstration, concise answer.
MYTH-FACT: make the misconception explicit and present a verified correction.
THREE-FEATURES: one scene per benefit, same product continuity, final packshot.
BEFORE-AFTER: matching angle and conditions, accurate and nonmisleading difference.
DAY-IN-LIFE: show product used naturally across a short sequence.
CRAFTSMANSHIP: macro materials, hands at work, quality details.
SENSORY: close textures, fluid behavior, realistic foley.
SOCIAL-PROOF: approved customer footage, authentic attribution, factual claims.
COMPARISON: matched test conditions and documented criteria.
TEASER: silhouette, partial reveal, date added later in editing.
LAUNCH: hero reveal, key differentiator, clear availability and CTA.
EDUCATIONAL: teach a small useful concept and connect it naturally to the product.
