# Creative testing frameworks

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for creative testing frameworks in AI video advertising.

## 1. Hook test

**How to use:** Change first three seconds while keeping audience, offer and landing page fixed.

**Example:** Compare 3-second hold and qualified view rate.

## 2. Visual style test

**How to use:** Compare macro studio vs natural lifestyle demonstration.

**Example:** Track click-through and conversion.

## 3. CTA test

**How to use:** Compare approved action wording without changing the offer.

**Example:** Measure post-click conversion.

## 4. Length test

**How to use:** Test 6, 15 and 30 seconds with equivalent messages.

**Example:** Compare completion and cost per result.

## 5. Fatigue monitoring

**How to use:** Watch performance over time by creative and audience.

**Example:** Refresh when evidence supports decline.

## 6. Experiment log

**How to use:** Record hypothesis, variables, dates, spend and results.

**Example:** Do not call differences significant without enough data.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using hook test.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
