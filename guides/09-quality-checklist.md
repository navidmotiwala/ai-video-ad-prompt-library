# Quality checklist

Review every video before publishing.

## Product accuracy

- [ ] **Product shape:** Model, proportions and materials match the real product.
- [ ] **Brand assets:** Logos, labels and packaging are accurate.
- [ ] **Specifications:** No invented features, certifications or test results.
- [ ] **Pricing:** Prices and offers are current and approved.

## Visual and audio quality

- [ ] **Motion:** No unnatural movement, deformation or broken physics.
- [ ] **Continuity:** Lighting, product appearance and scene direction are consistent.
- [ ] **Text:** Captions and legal copy are checked in the final edit.
- [ ] **Audio:** Speech, sound effects and music are synchronized and licensed.
- [ ] **Format:** Aspect ratio, duration and safe areas match the destination platform.

## Commercial compliance

- [ ] **Claims:** Medical, financial, performance and safety claims are supported.
- [ ] **Testimonials:** Reviews and customer stories are genuine and permission-based.
- [ ] **Consent:** People and voices are used with appropriate authorization.
- [ ] **Disclosure:** Synthetic content is labeled where required.
- [ ] **Advertising rules:** Relevant local laws and platform policies are checked.
- [ ] **Comparisons:** Before-and-after and competitor comparisons are fair and verifiable.

**Note:** AI generation cannot guarantee perfect product geometry, typography or factual accuracy.
