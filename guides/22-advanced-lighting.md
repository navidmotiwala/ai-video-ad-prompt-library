# Advanced lighting setups

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for advanced lighting setups in AI video advertising.

## 1. Three-point lighting

**How to use:** Key shapes product, fill controls shadows and backlight separates edges.

**Example:** Headphones on neutral pedestal.

## 2. Glossy surfaces

**How to use:** Use large diffused sources and reflection cards instead of random specular highlights.

**Example:** Smartphone glass and chrome.

## 3. Transparent objects

**How to use:** Backlight and edge lighting reveal contours while preserving transparency.

**Example:** Perfume glass bottle.

## 4. Low-key luxury

**How to use:** Narrow key and controlled rim lights isolate premium materials.

**Example:** Mechanical watch.

## 5. High-key retail

**How to use:** Broad soft light minimizes distracting shadows.

**Example:** White skincare packaging.

## 6. Practical neon

**How to use:** Visible colored fixtures create believable motivated lighting.

**Example:** Gaming controller on desk.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using three-point lighting.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
