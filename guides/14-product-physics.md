# Product physics and simulations

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for product physics and simulations in AI video advertising.

## 1. Water

**How to use:** Define source, gravity, surface tension and splash scale.

**Example:** Cold can meets a shallow water splash; droplets settle realistically.

## 2. Glass

**How to use:** Preserve thickness, transparency, refraction and realistic reflections.

**Example:** Perfume bottle rotates under controlled studio light.

## 3. Metal

**How to use:** Keep rigid shape and material-specific specular highlights.

**Example:** Chrome headphones rotate with crisp edge reflections.

## 4. Fabric

**How to use:** Specify weight, seams, wind and movement direction.

**Example:** Silk scarf drapes over handbag without clipping.

## 5. Smoke

**How to use:** Define direction, density, source and dissipation.

**Example:** Thin vapor curls behind perfume bottle, leaving label clear.

## 6. Collision

**How to use:** Objects contact and settle with plausible weight.

**Example:** Coffee beans bounce into a ceramic bowl and come to rest.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using water.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
