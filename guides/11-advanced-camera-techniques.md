# Advanced camera techniques

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for advanced camera techniques in AI video advertising.

## 1. Dolly zoom

**How to use:** Move camera forward while zooming out to keep the product the same apparent size; background perspective changes.

**Example:** Premium car in a tunnel; steady car scale, expanding tunnel perspective.

## 2. Arc shot

**How to use:** Travel along a curved path around a stationary subject.

**Example:** Sneaker on pedestal; reveal sole, stitching and heel.

## 3. Impossible camera pass

**How to use:** Simulate a camera traveling through a narrow opening.

**Example:** Camera enters a headphone earcup and emerges into a music studio.

## 4. Virtual crane

**How to use:** Rise from a close detail to an overhead reveal.

**Example:** Start on watch crown, rise above a table with full collection.

## 5. Lens handoff

**How to use:** Match composition between macro and wide shots.

**Example:** Macro coffee crema to full breakfast scene.

## 6. Focus pull

**How to use:** Shift attention between foreground and background without moving product.

**Example:** Focus from perfume cap to bottle label.
