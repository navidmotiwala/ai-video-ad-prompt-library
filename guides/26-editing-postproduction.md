# Editing and post-production

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for editing and post-production in AI video advertising.

## 1. Assembly

**How to use:** Build rough cut from approved short clips.

**Example:** Organize by hook, demo, proof, CTA.

## 2. Stabilization

**How to use:** Correct unwanted shake without cropping important features.

**Example:** Smooth tracking shot.

## 3. Color grading

**How to use:** Match exposure, contrast and color temperature across clips.

**Example:** Consistent warm product palette.

## 4. Upscaling

**How to use:** Use cautiously and inspect for invented texture.

**Example:** Check logos and fine print.

## 5. Frame interpolation

**How to use:** Inspect motion artifacts and edge distortions.

**Example:** Fast-moving hands and water.

## 6. Compositing

**How to use:** Add real UI, product labels, legal copy and CTA.

**Example:** Keep text readable on mobile.

## 7. Captions

**How to use:** Use accurate timing and safe margins.

**Example:** Review all languages.

## 8. Export QC

**How to use:** Check aspect ratio, audio levels, codec and platform specifications.

**Example:** Watch exported file in full.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using assembly.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
