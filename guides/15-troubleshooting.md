# AI video prompt troubleshooting

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for ai video prompt troubleshooting in AI video advertising.

## 1. Product changes shape

**How to use:** Use one reference product, fewer camera moves, shorter clip and clear geometry constraint.

**Example:** The bottle must retain identical cap, label and dimensions in every frame.

## 2. Logo or text is wrong

**How to use:** Generate textless footage and overlay approved graphics during editing.

**Example:** Leave the center of the label unobstructed for compositing.

## 3. Camera motion is chaotic

**How to use:** Specify one camera movement and stable subject action.

**Example:** Locked product; camera dollies in slowly over six seconds.

## 4. Hands look unnatural

**How to use:** Reduce hand interaction, use close references and check each frame.

**Example:** One hand picks up the mug by its handle with natural grip.

## 5. Inconsistent lighting

**How to use:** Specify key direction, time of day and exposure for all shots.

**Example:** Soft key from camera left, neutral fill and warm practical background.

## 6. Unrealistic physics

**How to use:** Describe cause, gravity, contact and final resting state.

**Example:** Ice cubes drop into water, splash, float and settle.

## 7. Character drift

**How to use:** Reuse approved reference images, wardrobe and shot descriptions.

**Example:** Same presenter, blue shirt, hair and background across clips.

## 8. Bad ending

**How to use:** Define a clear final composition or first/last frame.

**Example:** End with stable front-facing product and negative space for CTA.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using product changes shape.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
