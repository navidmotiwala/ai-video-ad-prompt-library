# Product transformation effects

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for product transformation effects in AI video advertising.

## 1. Particle assembly

**How to use:** Loose particles form a recognizable product.

**Example:** Thousands of brushed-metal particles converge into a smartwatch.

## 2. Liquid reveal

**How to use:** Fluid wraps around a product without hiding its branding.

**Example:** Transparent water ribbon circles a cold beverage can.

## 3. Material morph

**How to use:** Surface changes from one material to another while silhouette remains fixed.

**Example:** Shoe prototype switches from clay to finished fabric.

## 4. Mechanical assembly

**How to use:** Separate parts converge in correct order.

**Example:** Camera body, lens and dials assemble from an exploded view.

## 5. Portal transition

**How to use:** Product passes through a visual doorway into a new setting.

**Example:** Suitcase crosses a luminous doorway from studio to airport.

## 6. Scale shift

**How to use:** Move between miniature and real-world scale.

**Example:** Tiny coffee beans expand into a full café environment.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using particle assembly.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
