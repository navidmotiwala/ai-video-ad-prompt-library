# Getting started

Use this guide to build a clear AI product video prompt.

## Prompt formula

**Product** + **Objective** + **Setting** + **Action** + **Camera** + **Lighting** + **Timing** + **Audio** + **Format** + **Accuracy rules**

## Copyable example

```text
A premium matte-black wireless headphone rests on a dark stone pedestal. The camera slowly dollies forward while a narrow rim light reveals the curved earcups. Fine atmospheric haze moves behind the product. At second four the headphones rotate 30 degrees, exposing the controls. Natural studio reflections, accurate geometry, luxury commercial finish, 9:16, eight seconds. Soft mechanical click and restrained electronic ambience. Preserve product proportions and branding.
```

## Workflow

1. **Choose the product:** Use the real model, color and materials.
2. **Set one objective:** Awareness, product demonstration or conversion.
3. **Choose the shot:** Reveal, orbit, close-up or real-world use.
4. **Add production details:** Camera movement, lighting, duration and aspect ratio.
5. **Generate short clips:** Keep each shot focused on one action.
6. **Edit and verify:** Add approved branding, text and audio after generation.
