## Core prompt grammar

Template: [product identity] + [advertising objective] + [scene] + [product action] + [camera movement] + [lighting] + [material behavior] + [timing] + [sound] + [output format] + [continuity rules].
Example: A premium matte-black wireless headphone rests on a dark stone pedestal. The camera slowly dollies forward while a narrow rim light reveals the curved earcups. Fine atmospheric haze moves behind the product. At second four the headphones rotate 30 degrees, exposing the controls. Natural studio reflections, accurate geometry, luxury commercial finish, 9:16, eight seconds. Soft mechanical click and restrained electronic ambience. Preserve product proportions and branding.

### Recommended process

1. Choose one product and one ad objective.
2. Select one combination shortcut.
3. Convert the shortcuts into normal descriptive language.
4. Generate short individual shots, not an entire complex commercial at once.
5. Add verified brand text, logos, prices and calls to action in an editor.
6. Review product accuracy before publishing.
