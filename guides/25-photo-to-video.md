# Product photography to video

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for product photography to video in AI video advertising.

## 1. Source capture

**How to use:** Photograph exact product with even lighting and sharp label.

**Example:** Front, side and detail images.

## 2. Clean background

**How to use:** Remove distracting elements without altering product geometry.

**Example:** Neutral product cutout.

## 3. Motion brief

**How to use:** Animate camera or environment rather than product identity.

**Example:** Slow 20-degree orbit.

## 4. Reference lock

**How to use:** State immutable parts such as seams, controls and label.

**Example:** Preserve cap and bottle shape.

## 5. Generate variants

**How to use:** Make short clips with one motion each.

**Example:** Push-in, orbit and light sweep.

## 6. Composite and review

**How to use:** Overlay verified branding and check frames for drift.

**Example:** Export clean product master.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using source capture.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
