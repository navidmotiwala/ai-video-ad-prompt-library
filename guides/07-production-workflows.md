## Advanced production workflows



### Workflow A: TEXT-TO-VIDEO

define objective and target audience; select one scene; write physical actions; choose framing, lens and lighting; specify duration and aspect ratio; generate variations; review geometry and motion; edit; add typography and licensed sound; export.


### Workflow B: IMAGE-TO-VIDEO

photograph or render exact product; clean source image; lock label and geometry; prompt primarily motion; animate one action per shot; check drift frame by frame; composite verified brand assets.


### Workflow C: PRODUCT-TO-VIDEO

shoot product from front, back, side and macro; create reference sheet; write shot list; generate each shot separately; edit matching angles; verify materials and logos; finalize packshot.


### Workflow D: UGC

write natural hook; film or generate disclosed creator footage; demonstrate real product use; record authentic narration; add captions; avoid fake testimonials.


### Workflow E: MULTI-SHOT

write beat sheet; create consistent character/product references; generate opening and closing frames for each shot; produce individual clips; match lighting and screen direction; edit with audio continuity.


### Workflow F: LOCALIZATION

export textless master; replace voiceover, captions, pricing, offers and regulatory text with approved localized versions; verify cultural context.
WORKFLOW G A/B TEST: hold audience and offer constant; vary only hook, framing or first-three-second action; compare thumb-stop, watch time, click-through and conversion using sufficient sample size.


### Workflow H: AUTOMATION

keep structured shot briefs in CSV or JSON; batch render where platform permits; log seed, model, prompt, source assets and license; review outputs manually; render through editor.
WORKFLOW I QUALITY CONTROL: verify product geometry, hands, text, logos, shadows, reflections, physics, continuity, frame-safe regions, audio sync, disclosure and claims before publishing.
