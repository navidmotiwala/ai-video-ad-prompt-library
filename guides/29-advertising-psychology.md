# Advertising psychology and hooks

[← Back to library](../README.md)

**Purpose:** Practical reference for advertising psychology and hooks in AI video advertising.

## 1. Curiosity

**How to use:** Reveal an unusual product detail, then explain it clearly.

**Example:** What makes this zipper different?.

## 2. Problem recognition

**How to use:** Show familiar friction without exaggeration.

**Example:** Messy desk before organizer demo.

## 3. Concrete demonstration

**How to use:** Show a real action rather than an unsupported promise.

**Example:** Suitcase wheels rolling over different surfaces.

## 4. Objection handling

**How to use:** Answer a specific concern with accurate evidence.

**Example:** Show verified size and weight.

## 5. Social proof

**How to use:** Use real attributed feedback with permission.

**Example:** Customer describes their own experience.

## 6. Clarity

**How to use:** One benefit, one visual idea and one CTA.

**Example:** Avoid overcrowded overlays.

## Reusable prompt pattern

```text
Create a product advertisement for [EXACT PRODUCT] using curiosity.
Scene: [SETTING]. Action: [ONE PHYSICAL ACTION]. Camera: [ONE MOVEMENT].
Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Duration: [SECONDS]. Aspect ratio: [RATIO].
Preserve product geometry, packaging and approved brand identity.
Use only verified product claims. Add text and logos in post-production.
```

**Production check:** Review the final video frame by frame for geometry, physics, continuity, claims and licensing.
